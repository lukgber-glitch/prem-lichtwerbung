## Product-Specific Verification

### Lightboxes (Products 1-2)
- ✅ Shows **flat panel** lightbox (not 3D letters)
- ✅ Rectangular or square shape
- ✅ Aluminum frame visible
- ✅ Even backlighting when illuminated

### Channel Letters (Products 3-4)
- ✅ Shows **3D dimensional individual letters**
- ✅ NOT flat lightbox panels
- ✅ Letters have depth/dimension
- ✅ Mounted separately with standoffs

### LED Neon Signs (Products 5-6)
- ✅ Shows **flexible tube-style lighting**
- ✅ NOT rigid channel letters
- ✅ Neon tube aesthetic (continuous tubes)
- ✅ Colorful glow effect

### Pylon Signs (Products 7-8)
- ✅ Shows **tall freestanding structure**
- ✅ NOT building-mounted signs
- ✅ Single post or monument base
- ✅ 15-30 feet tall appearance

### Window Graphics (Products 9-10)
- ✅ Applied **on glass windows**
- ✅ NOT exterior wall signs
- ✅ Frosted or printed vinyl visible
- ✅ On storefront or office windows

## Troubleshooting PromeAI

### Issue: Wrong Product Type Generated

**Solution**: Add more specific keywords to prompt:
- For lightboxes: Add "flat panel", "backlit panel"
- For channel letters: Add "3D dimensional", "individual letters"
- For neon: Add "flexible tube lighting", "tube-style neon"
- For pylons: Add "freestanding", "tall monument structure"

### Issue: Generic Building Without Product

**Solution**: Emphasize product in prompt:
- Start with "Professional product photography of..."
- Add "commercial signage product photo"
- Use "close-up of" or "detailed shot of"

### Issue: Low Quality or Blurry

**Solution**:
- Increase quality setting to HD or Ultra HD
- Add "4K quality", "high resolution" to prompt
- Generate multiple variations and pick best

### Issue: Wrong Style (Vintage/Retro)

**Solution**:
- Add "modern", "contemporary" to prompt
- Specify "LED" technology explicitly
- Add "professional commercial photography"
