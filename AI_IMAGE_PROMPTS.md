## AI Generation Tips

### For Best Results

1. **Specify "signage" or "commercial signage product"** - Ensures AI focuses on the sign itself, not just buildings
2. **Include "product photography" for primary images** - Creates isolated product shots on neutral backgrounds
3. **Use "3D" and "dimensional" for channel letters** - Emphasizes depth and individual letter mounting
4. **Specify "flexible tube lighting" for neon** - Differentiates LED neon from rigid channel letters
5. **Use "flat panel" or "lightbox" explicitly** - Ensures AI generates actual lightbox products
6. **Include "freestanding" for pylons** - Distinguishes from building-mounted signs
7. **Specify "on glass" for window graphics** - Ensures application to windows, not walls

### Common AI Mistakes to Avoid

- Generic buildings without visible signage
- Construction workers or installation crews in frame
- Wrong product type (e.g., neon when requesting lightbox)
- Vintage or retro signage instead of modern LED
- Indoor scenes when outdoor is needed
- No visible product in "primary" shots

### Recommended Settings

- **Resolution**: 1920x1080 or 1200x800 minimum
- **Aspect Ratio**: 16:9 or 3:2
- **Style**: Commercial photography, professional product photography
- **Quality**: High, 4K, photorealistic
- **Lighting**: Studio lighting (primary), natural/dramatic (day/night)
