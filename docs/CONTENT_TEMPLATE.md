## Product Photography Guidelines

### Required Images Checklist

Every product must have:
- ✅ **Day Image** (well-lit, bright, showing product turned off)
- ✅ **Night Image** (showing illumination, dramatic lighting)
- ✅ **Additional Images** (minimum 3-5 total):
  - Close-up details
  - Installation view
  - Size comparison
  - Different angles

### Day/Night Shot Requirements

#### Day Image
- **Lighting**: Natural daylight or bright studio lighting
- **Purpose**: Show product construction, materials, mounting
- **Background**: Clean, neutral (white or light gray preferred)
- **Focus**: Sharp detail on product craftsmanship

#### Night Image
- **Lighting**: Product illuminated, ambient lighting dimmed
- **Purpose**: Showcase illumination quality, glow effect, visibility
- **Background**: Darker setting to emphasize light output
- **Focus**: Capture glow, light uniformity, brand effect

### Photo Quality Checklist

- ✅ **Minimum Resolution**: 2000x2000 pixels
- ✅ **Format**: JPG (photos) or PNG (graphics/logos with transparency)
- ✅ **File Size**: Under 2MB (optimized for web)
- ✅ **Aspect Ratio**: 1:1 (square) or 4:3 (landscape) preferred
- ✅ **No Watermarks**: Clean, professional images only
- ✅ **Consistent Style**: Similar lighting and backgrounds across products

### Photography Angles

1. **Front View** (primary image) - Straight-on, centered
2. **Side View** - Show depth and mounting
3. **Close-Up** - Detail of illumination, materials
4. **Installation View** - Mounted on wall/building (if possible)
5. **Scale Reference** - Product next to common object for size context

## SEO Guidelines

### Meta Titles

**Format**: `[Product/Category Name] | [Key Benefit] | Prem-Lichtwerbung`

**Length**: 50-60 characters (including spaces)

**Examples**:
- `Premium LED Lightboxes | 24/7 Brand Visibility | Prem-Lichtwerbung`
- `Custom Channel Letters | 3D Illuminated Signage | Prem-Lichtwerbung`
- `LED Neon Signs | Modern Neon Lighting | Prem-Lichtwerbung`

### Meta Descriptions

**Length**: 150-160 characters (including spaces)

**Format**: `[Action verb] [product/category] [key benefit]. [Value prop]. [Call-to-action]`

**Examples**:
- `Discover premium LED lightboxes that illuminate your brand 24/7. Energy-efficient, weather-resistant, professional installation. Get a free quote today!`
- `Transform your storefront with custom 3D channel letters. Individually illuminated, fully customizable, installation included. Contact us for design consultation.`

### Keywords Strategy

**Primary Keywords** (use in title, first paragraph, H1):
- Product/category name
- Primary material/technology (LED, neon, aluminum)
- Main benefit/feature

**Secondary Keywords** (use naturally throughout):
- Related terms and synonyms
- Application contexts (storefront, outdoor, commercial)
- Brand attributes (custom, premium, professional)

**Long-Tail Keywords** (use in headings and descriptions):
- "custom LED lightbox for storefront"
- "weather-resistant channel letters outdoor"
- "energy-efficient illuminated signage"

### Alt Text for Images

**Format**: `[Product name] - [Key visual detail] - [Context/benefit]`

**Examples**:
- `Premium LED Lightbox - Single-sided illuminated display - Storefront signage`
- `Custom Channel Letters - 3D illuminated building signage - Night view`
- `LED Neon Sign - Custom logo design - Vibrant blue glow effect`

## Image File Naming Convention

### Format

```
[product-category]_[product-name]_[image-type]_[variant].[extension]
```

### Examples

```
lightbox_premium-single-sided_day_front.jpg
lightbox_premium-single-sided_night_front.jpg
lightbox_premium-single-sided_detail_led-strip.jpg
lightbox_premium-single-sided_installed_storefront.jpg

channel-letters_3d-led_day_front.jpg
channel-letters_3d-led_night_illuminated.jpg
channel-letters_3d-led_detail_mounting.jpg

neon-sign_custom-logo_night_glow.jpg
neon-sign_custom-logo_day_unlit.jpg
```

### Image Types

- `day` - Well-lit, product off
- `night` - Dimly lit, product on/illuminated
- `detail` - Close-up of specific feature
- `installed` - Product in real-world setting
- `diagram` - Technical drawing or specification
- `mockup` - Design visualization

### File Naming Rules

- All lowercase
- Use hyphens (-) to separate words within segments
- Use underscores (_) to separate segments
- Be descriptive but concise
- No spaces or special characters
- Include relevant keywords

## Video Content Guidelines

### Product Demonstration Videos

**Duration**: 30-90 seconds

**Content Structure**:
1. **Opening** (5 sec): Product name and key benefit on screen
2. **Day View** (10 sec): Product in well-lit environment, show construction
3. **Night View** (15 sec): Product illuminated, emphasize glow and visibility
4. **Installation/Use** (20 sec): Show mounting, size scale, real-world context
5. **Closing** (5 sec): Logo, website, call-to-action

**Technical Specs**:
- Resolution: 1920x1080 (Full HD) minimum
- Format: MP4 (H.264 codec)
- Frame rate: 30fps or 60fps
- Aspect ratio: 16:9 (landscape) or 9:16 (vertical for social)

### Installation Tutorial Videos

**Duration**: 2-5 minutes

**Content Structure**:
1. Introduction and tools required (30 sec)
2. Step-by-step installation process (3-4 min)
3. Safety tips and best practices (30 sec)
4. Final result and maintenance tips (30 sec)

**Production Tips**:
- Use clear, well-lit close-up shots
- Add text overlays for key steps
- Include safety warnings prominently
- Background music should be subtle
- Add chapter markers for long videos

### Video Hosting

- **Primary**: YouTube (embed on product pages)
- **Secondary**: Vimeo (higher quality for portfolio)
- **Social**: Instagram Reels, TikTok (short-form content)

## Content Best Practices Summary

### Writing Style
- ✅ Professional yet approachable tone
- ✅ Focus on benefits, not just features
- ✅ Use active voice and action verbs
- ✅ Include specific details and measurements
- ✅ Address customer pain points directly
- ✅ End with clear call-to-action

### Brand Voice
- **Confident**: We are experts in illuminated signage
- **Supportive**: We help businesses shine brighter
- **Professional**: Quality, reliability, attention to detail
- **Inspiring**: "Your brand deserves to shine"

### Quality Checklist
- ✅ No spelling or grammar errors
- ✅ Consistent formatting and style
- ✅ All technical specs verified
- ✅ Images optimized for web (compressed)
- ✅ Alt text and meta descriptions included
- ✅ Internal links to related products/categories
- ✅ Clear pricing or "request quote" CTA
