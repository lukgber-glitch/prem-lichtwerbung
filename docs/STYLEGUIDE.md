# Prem-Lichtwerbung Brand Style Guide

## Brand Identity

### Primary Colors
- Electric Blue: #00D9FF (main accent - represents illumination)
- Deep Navy: #0A1628 (backgrounds, headers)
- Warm White: #FFF8E7 (text on dark backgrounds)
- Pure White: #FFFFFF (main backgrounds)

### Secondary Colors
- Neon Pink: #FF006E (CTAs, special offers)
- Electric Yellow: #FFD600 (highlights, new badges)
- Soft Gray: #F5F5F5 (section backgrounds)
- Dark Gray: #2D3748 (body text)

### Typography
- Headings: Inter (bold, modern, clean)
- Body: Inter (regular, 16px base)
- Accent: Orbitron (for product names, LED-style feel)

### Design Mood
- Modern and clean
- Slightly playful with glow effects
- Professional but approachable
- Your brand deserves to shine energy

### Visual Elements
- Subtle glow effects on buttons and CTAs
- Card-based layouts with soft shadows
- Night-mode product images prominently featured
- Before/after sliders for case studies
- 3D-style mockup placeholders
